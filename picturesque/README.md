# Picturesque

## Vision

Picture a world painted in soft gouache and lit from within: the ray tracing exists to make light feel good rather than to simulate it faithfully, so sunlight is buttery and peach-gold, drifting to honey, coral, and rosy-lavender at the edges of the day, with shadows tinted a cool periwinkle or dusty mauve like colored-pencil shading instead of simple darkness. Bounce light is exaggerated on purpose, so grass blushes green onto neighboring stone and a red mushroom house warms the snow beside it, while soft, wide-penumbra shadows and warm, saturated ambient occlusion keep every crease cozy instead of gray. The grade lifts the blacks to deep plum or navy, leans slightly desaturated in the highlights and rich in the midtones like pigment on paper, and uses a gentle filmic shoulder and faint bloom so brights roll off like watercolor. Water becomes translucent aqua-to-lilac jelly with dreamy, blurred sky reflections and shimmering caustics, stained glass casts colored light across floors, skies are marshmallow-cloud gradients with backlit rims, and fog shifts by biome from lavender mornings to peachy dusks to minty forests, with dusty golden light shafts and floating motes filtering through leaves and windows. Night is deep indigo with silvery-periwinkle moonlight, but the real star is generous, amber lantern and fireplace glow that spills into soft pools and makes interiors feel unbelievably inviting, all while avoiding hard contrast, mirror-sharp reflections, sharpening, grain, and cold moody grading, with an optional whisper of paper-texture or softened distant edges for a hand-illustrated touch. If a screenshot’s first impression is “snug,” it’s working.
